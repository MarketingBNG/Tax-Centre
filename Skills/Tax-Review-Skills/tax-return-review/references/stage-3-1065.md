# Stage 3 — Form 1065 (partnerships and multi-member LLCs)

Run after Stage 1 and 2. Every line below produces a register entry. Where a
check needs a computed figure, the calculation layer supplies it.

## 3.1 Header and elections

| Check | Plain English |
|---|---|
| Page 1 boxes: initial / final / amended / name or address change / technical termination | Wrong box = wrong processing |
| Business code, accounting method, number of Schedules K-1 = number of partners at any time in the year | K-1 count must include partners who left |
| Schedule B questions — every one answered, not left default | Questions 3–4 (ownership of and by other entities), 6 (foreign partners), 10–11 (§754, like-kind), 14–15 (foreign accounts, 8865), 22–25 (partnership representative, BBA election, §163(j)) |
| Partnership representative named with US address and phone | Required; centralised audit regime |
| Foreign partner? | Schedule B "yes", §1446 withholding (8804/8805/8813), K-3 required, no §6698 waiver assumption |
| Eligible to elect out of BBA (100 or fewer partners, all eligible types)? | Election is annual and on the return; a trust or another partnership as partner blocks it |

## 3.2 Income and deductions (page 1 and Schedule K)

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Page 1 ordinary income agrees to books after Stage 1 adjustments and M-1 | Tie-out |
| Guaranteed payments on line 10 (not wages, not distributions); also on K line 4 and K-1 box 4 | Partners are not employees |
| Separately stated items pulled off page 1: interest, dividends, capital gains, §1231, charitable, §179, investment interest, foreign taxes | These must reach K-1 boxes separately |
| Rental activity on 8825, not page 1 | Passive character |
| Portfolio income on Schedule K, not page 1 | Character |
| Meals at deductible amount only; entertainment excluded | M-1 |
| Officer / partner health insurance as guaranteed payment, reported to partner | Deducted by partner, not partnership |
| Self-employment: K line 14A includes ordinary income and guaranteed payments for general partners / active LLC members; excludes limited partners' distributive share (position note if LLC members treated as limited) | Highest-audited K-1 box; `authority: verify` |
| §199A (QBI): K line 20 codes Z / AA populated — QBI, W-2 wages, UBIA — per activity | Missing = partner cannot claim deduction |
| §163(j): if gross receipts test failed, 8990 attached and excess business interest allocated on K-1 | Limitation |
| Foreign taxes paid, foreign source income — K-2/K-3 unless the domestic filing exception is documented | K-2/K-3 penalties are per K-1 |

## 3.3 Schedule L, M-1, M-2

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| L beginning balances = prior year L ending | IRS matching |
| L agrees to Stage 1 reconciled TB (book basis unless tax-basis books) | Tie-out |
| Partners' capital on L = total of K-1 item L ending capital = M-2 line 9 | Three places, one number |
| M-1: book income → tax income; every reconciling item named; net = page 1 line 22 ± separately stated | Tie-out |
| M-2: opening capital (tax basis) + contributions + net income per tax + other increases − distributions − other decreases = closing; agrees to sum of K-1s | Tax-basis capital is mandatory on K-1 item L; if books are GAAP, a separate tax-basis roll must exist |
| Question: did any partner's capital go negative? | Flag — deficit restoration / basis / at-risk question for the partner |

## 3.4 Schedules K-1

Check every K-1, not a sample.

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| Partner name, TIN, address, entity type, domestic/foreign — matches ownership schedule | Wrong TIN = rejected e-file or mismatch letter |
| Profit / loss / capital percentages: beginning and ending, agree to the operating agreement; sum to 100% | Allocation |
| Item J percentages reflect mid-year changes if any | Varying interests |
| Item K share of liabilities: recourse / non-recourse / qualified non-recourse, total = Schedule L liabilities | Affects partner basis and at-risk |
| Item L capital roll (tax basis) — see 3.3 | Mandatory |
| Box 1 through box 20: sum of all K-1 boxes = Schedule K line | The single most common e-file rejection |
| Box 4 guaranteed payments to the right partner | Individual |
| Box 14 SE — general partners only unless position note | See 3.2 |
| Box 16 / K-3 for foreign items | See 3.2 |
| Box 19 distributions = books, per partner | Ties to M-2 and to partner basis |
| Box 20 codes Z / AA / AB / AC / AH populated where applicable (QBI, UBIA, §163(j), §751, basis) | Partner needs them |
| Foreign partner: 8805 attached, withholding shown, K-3 attached | §1446 |
| Special allocations: only if the operating agreement allows and there is substantial economic effect; note the position | `authority: verify` |

## 3.6 State

- Every state in which the partnership has nexus (from obligation engine) has a return or a documented "no filing" reason.
- Composite / PTE tax elections: made or declined deliberately, and shown on K-1 state supplements.
- Non-resident partner withholding: computed by Drake, agrees to state schedule.
- Apportionment: sales / payroll / property factors agree to books.

## 3.7 Sign-off items specific to 1065

1. Total of all K-1 box 1 = Schedule K line 1 = page 1 line 22 (after separately stated).
2. Total K-1 ending capital = Schedule L partners' capital = M-2 line 9.
3. Total K-1 distributions = M-2 line 6 = books.
4. Total K-1 liabilities = Schedule L total liabilities.
5. Number of K-1s = Schedule B count.
6. Every Schedule B "yes" has its form attached.
