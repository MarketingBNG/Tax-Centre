# Stage 3 — Form 1120-F and Form 5472

Two different situations that are easy to confuse. Get the situation right
first (Stage 0), then run the matching section.

| Situation | Return | 5472? |
|---|---|---|
| Foreign corporation (e.g., Indian Pvt Ltd) with US trade or business, branch, or US-source income | 1120-F | Yes if reportable transactions with related parties |
| US corporation (1120) with a 25%+ foreign shareholder | 1120 | Yes — one per related party with transactions |
| US single-member LLC owned by a foreign person or entity (disregarded) | Pro-forma 1120 + 5472 | Yes — always if any transaction with owner |
| US LLC taxed as partnership with foreign partners | 1065 | No 5472; §1446 withholding instead |
| Foreign corporation with only FDAP income, fully withheld at source | Often no 1120-F needed, but a protective 1120-F may be advisable | Position note |

## A. Form 5472 (in every context)

The penalty is per form, per year, and does not depend on tax due. Treat every
5472 finding as High minimum.

| Check | Plain English |
|---|---|
| One 5472 per foreign related party that had any reportable transaction | Not one form for everyone |
| Part I: reporting corporation EIN, total assets, principal business, country of incorporation of foreign owner; for a DRE, the "foreign-owned US DE" box | Header |
| Part II: 25% foreign shareholder identified — name, address, US TIN if any, foreign TIN, country | Both direct and ultimate indirect owner if different |
| Part III: related party — name, address, TIN, relationship, principal business | One per form |
| Part IV: monetary transactions — sales, purchases, rents, royalties, services, commissions, interest, **loans (opening and closing balances), capital contributions, distributions** — agree to the ledger by category | Reviewer ties each box to a GL account. Zero where nothing; do not leave blank |
| Part V (DRE only): contributions and distributions with the owner, including formation funding and owner-paid expenses | Owner paying an LLC bill from a personal card is a contribution — reportable |
| Part VI: non-monetary / less-than-full consideration transactions described | Free use of office, unpaid services |
| Part VII: base erosion payments (if BEAT applies) | Large taxpayers only |
| Part VIII: cost-sharing | If any |
| Filed **with** the 1120 / pro-forma 1120 by the due date including extension; DRE pro-forma has a specific filing method — confirm the method in the firm's SOP for the current year | Stand-alone e-file is not available for the DRE pro-forma in all years |
| Foreign owner's US TIN: obtained or "applied for" / "none" stated consistently with 1120 Schedule K | Consistency |
| Amounts agree to the India-side records: TP study, Form 3CEB, FEMA APR / ODI filing, invoices, TDS certificates | Same transaction, both sides — divergence is a defect or a documented position |
| Records maintenance: books sufficient to support the reported amounts are kept and accessible | Statutory record-keeping condition |

## B. Form 1120-F

| Check | Plain English |
|---|---|
| Section I vs Section II: FDAP income not effectively connected (withheld at 30% or treaty rate, 1042-S received) vs income effectively connected with a US trade or business | Wrong section = wrong tax |
| Treaty position: if reduced rate or no-PE claim under the US-India treaty, **8833 attached** with article cited from the treaty text in the corpus | Treaty claim without 8833 carries its own penalty; `authority: verify` |
| Permanent establishment analysis documented if claiming no ECI | Position register |
| Branch profits tax (Section III): dividend-equivalent amount computed by calculation layer; treaty rate applied only with 8833 | Second-level tax; `authority: verify` |
| Branch-level interest tax if interest deducted exceeds interest paid | Section III |
| Expense allocation to ECI: interest (§1.882-5 method chosen and documented), head-office expenses allocated with a stated method and support | Allocation method must be consistent year to year |
| Schedule L, M-1, M-2 for the US branch books only; agrees to branch TB; reconciles to the foreign parent's audited financials (segment) | Tie-out |
| Schedule P (partnership interests), Schedule H (home office expense allocation), Schedule I (interest allocation) as applicable | Attachments |
| Estimated tax, 1042-S credits, withholding credits agree to received forms | Payments |
| Protective return box if filing to preserve deductions while claiming no ECI | Deliberate |
| Timely filing: deductions are lost if the return is filed late beyond the grace period — flag any late 1120-F as Critical | `authority: verify` |
| State: branch income taxed by states without treaty protection — obligation engine confirms | States do not follow treaties |
| Foreign parent's Indian compliance: Form 15CA/CB, TDS on US branch remittances, POEM, India transfer pricing — symmetry check only; not this review's scope | Flag for the India team |

## C. Foreign-owned single-member LLC (pro-forma 1120 + 5472)

| Check | Plain English |
|---|---|
| Pro-forma 1120: name, address, EIN of the LLC; "foreign-owned US DE" indicated; no income or deductions on the pro-forma itself | It is a cover sheet for the 5472 |
| 5472 Parts I–V fully populated per section A above | Core |
| Owner's own US filing: if the LLC has ECI, the foreign owner (individual → 1040-NR; corporation → 1120-F) reports the LLC's income directly | The LLC does not pay the tax — the owner does; a missing owner return is Critical |
| State: LLC franchise / annual report / state income tax where the LLC has nexus | Obligation engine |
| FBAR / 8938 if the LLC holds foreign accounts | Information returns |
| India side: LLC ownership by an Indian resident is ODI / OPI — confirm FEMA reporting exists (UIN, APR); by an Indian company — ODI and Form FC | Run `fema-odi-review`; unresolved ODI is a client-level open item, not a US return item |

## D. Withholding checks that ride along with these returns

| Payment | Form | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Interest, royalties, services, dividends paid by a US entity to a foreign related party | 1042 / 1042-S, W-8BEN-E on file | Withheld at 30% or treaty rate with valid W-8 and treaty article stated; agrees to 5472 Part IV and to the expense on the return |
| Partnership ECI to foreign partner | 8804 / 8805 / 8813 | Quarterly instalments, annual return |
| Real property dispositions | 8288 / 8288-A | If any |
| Wages to foreign persons | Payroll — W-2 or 1042-S depending on status | Classification |
